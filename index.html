Our story.

We’re mom and dad. Kora began as an app for our own family. We wanted one place for the small details of our baby’s day, so we could keep track together with the people caring for our baby.

Now we’re preparing to share Kora with other families. It brings sleep, feeds, diapers and family notes into a shared record on iPhone. It comes from our experience as parents, not clinical expertise. It helps you keep a record; it does not tell you what is right for your child.
