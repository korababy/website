Launch list privacy

Marysol Longo operates Kora Baby. You can contact us at support@korababy.app or Marysol Longo, Postfach, 8600 Dübendorf 1, Switzerland.

What we collect

If you join the launch list, we collect your email address and whether you confirmed it. We use it only to send the confirmation email and news directly related to Kora Baby’s launch. A launch-list signup does not create a Kora account or reserve a first-100 family place. We do not ask for details about your baby.

Who handles it

We store the list in Kora’s Supabase project. Resend sends the confirmation and launch emails for us and receives the address and email content needed to deliver them. The website itself is hosted on GitHub Pages, which processes technical request data to deliver the page. We do not add advertising pixels or cross-site tracking to this signup page.

Leaving the list

Every launch-list email will include an unsubscribe link. You can also ask us to remove your address at support@korababy.app. We will close and remove the launch list after the launch campaign; unconfirmed addresses will be removed sooner. You may ask what we hold about you or request correction or deletion through the same address.

For the app’s separate privacy policy, see Kora Baby privacy.
